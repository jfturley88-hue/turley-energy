# PlanitBER — Value Proposition & Use Cases

**Independent Indicative BOQ Platform for Ireland's Residential Energy Upgrade Market**
Irish market rates Q2 2026 · SCSI Cost Plan format · SEAI grant-aware

---

## What It Does

PlanitBER generates an itemised, indicative Bill of Quantities (BOQ) from DEAP survey geometry and a works selector. In under two minutes it produces:

- A section-by-section cost plan with material rates, labour rates (county-adjusted), and VAT
- Automatic SEAI grant deductions (Better Energy Homes or One Stop Shop) with per-measure breakdown
- Net cost to homeowner after grants, with green loan monthly guide
- Estimated annual energy saving and payback years (from BER improvement)
- A printable/PDF document in SCSI Cost Plan format suitable for bank and grant submissions

The BOQ is **independent** — derived from DEAP geometry, not prepared by the contractor. This is the core trust feature.

## Use Cases

### 1. Homeowners Planning Energy Upgrades
**Primary audience.** A homeowner wants to know: "What will this actually cost me, and what will I save?"
- Instant indicative budget before engaging contractors
- Clear gross vs. net-of-grant cost breakdown per measure
- Annual energy savings estimate and payback years
- Green loan monthly repayment guide
- Protection against grant inflation: the BOQ shows what works *should* cost independently of any contractor quote
- Actual vs. estimated budget tracker as works progress (Quoted € / Actual € columns)

### 2. Banks & Credit Unions — Green Loan Applications
Banks issuing green mortgages and eco loans need a credible cost document.
- SCSI Cost Plan format provides the professional structure lenders expect
- Gross → SEAI grants → net-to-finance summary on the cover page
- Monthly repayment guide at 4% over 10 years
- "Independent indicative estimate" language distinguishes it from contractor quotes
- BER improvement pathway (e.g. D1 → B2) quantifies the asset upgrade value
- AIB, Bank of Ireland, and credit union green loan products all require cost evidence

### 3. BER Assessors
A BER assessor visits a home, produces a DEAP report, and recommends an upgrade path. Today there is no fast way to turn that recommendation into a budget.
- Assessor copies DEAP geometry into PlanitBER, selects recommended measures, and generates an indicative cost during or immediately after the assessment visit
- Client leaves with a budget alongside their BER certificate — dramatically increasing the likelihood of action
- Creates a direct link between the assessor's recommendation and the homeowner's financial decision
- Supports SEAI's goal of increasing conversion from BER assessment to upgrade works

### 4. Contractors & Energy Upgrade Companies
Contractors tendering for SEAI works need a reference to confirm scope and provide competitive quotes.
- The BOQ serves as a structured scope-of-works document they can price against
- Itemised sections by trade (insulation, heating, electrical, windows) support sub-contractor coordination
- Published SEO 2024 / SCSI 2025–26 rate basis means contractors can benchmark their quotes
- Helps reputable contractors demonstrate value vs. cheaper, non-registered operators

### 5. One Stop Shops (OSS)
SEAI-registered One Stop Shops coordinate all contractors, grants, and inspections on behalf of homeowners.
- PlanitBER provides an instant feasibility cost before an OSS engages a surveyor
- Helps OSS advisors have credible cost conversations with prospective clients at initial contact
- Can be used to pre-qualify projects before committing full survey costs

### 6. Local Authorities & Approved Housing Bodies (AHBs)
Retrofitting social housing stock at scale requires rapid batch estimation.
- A housing officer can generate indicative costs for each property type in their estate in minutes
- Programme budget forecasting before tendering
- Supports Department of Housing retrofitting obligations under the National Retrofitting Plan (NRP)
- Provides audit trail for grant applications at scale

### 7. SEAI Programme Officers
SEAI designs grant rates, monitors programme uptake, and evaluates value-for-money.
- Aggregated anonymous project cost data would give SEAI real-market cost intelligence
- Helps calibrate grant rates against actual Irish market costs (not just academic models)
- Identifies outlier projects where costs are unusually high (potential fraud signal)
- Supports programme evaluation and impact reporting

### 8. Estate Agents & Property Solicitors
From 2026, BER ratings increasingly affect property values and mortgage eligibility.
- Agents can show prospective buyers: "Upgrade to B2 costs ~€X net of grants"
- Solicitors can include upgrade cost disclosure in property transaction documentation
- Supports informed property decisions as the green mortgage market grows

### 9. Energy Advisors & Retrofit Coordinators
Private energy advisors (not SEAI assessors) help homeowners navigate the retrofit journey.
- Instant cost modelling for different upgrade scenarios (e.g. ASHP-only vs. full deep retrofit)
- Supports client education and project sequencing advice
- Differentiates advisors who can provide budget context vs. those who cannot

### 10. Mortgage Advisors & Financial Planners
Green mortgages (lower rates for B-rated or better homes) are a growing product.
- Advisors can model the investment case: upgrade cost vs. mortgage rate saving vs. energy saving
- Monthly repayment guide at green loan rates
- BER improvement pathway quantifies the collateral value increase

## Why This Matters for SEAI

Ireland has committed to retrofitting **500,000 homes to B2 or better** by 2030 under the Climate Action Plan. Current annual completion rates are significantly behind target.

**Three barriers to uptake that PlanitBER directly addresses:**

1. **Cost uncertainty** — Homeowners don't act because they don't know what retrofit will cost. PlanitBER provides an instant, credible answer.

2. **Grant inflation distrust** — A widespread perception that contractors simply add the grant value to their price, leaving homeowners no better off. PlanitBER's independent BOQ shows what works *should* cost before any contractor is engaged, giving homeowners a benchmark.

3. **Friction between assessment and action** — A BER certificate recommends measures but provides no budget. The gap between "you need wall insulation" and "here's what that costs and here's what you'll save" is where most homeowners stall. PlanitBER closes that gap at the point of assessment.
