# PlanitBER — Value Proposition

**An independent, instant Bill of Quantities for Ireland's home retrofit market.**
Turns a BER certificate into an actionable budget in under two minutes.

---

## The Problem

Ireland has committed to retrofitting **500,000 homes to B2 or better by 2030**.
Current completion rates are well behind target. Three demand-side barriers explain why:

**1. Cost uncertainty stops homeowners acting.**
A BER assessment recommends measures but provides no budget. Homeowners don't know if an upgrade will cost €8,000 or €80,000. Without a credible number they don't apply for grants, don't approach banks, and don't proceed. SEAI spends heavily on awareness — the conversion from "interested" to "committed" breaks down at the budget question.

**2. Grant inflation erodes trust in the scheme.**
A widespread perception — well-founded in some cases — that contractors price to the gross figure and pocket the grant, leaving the homeowner no better off. This actively suppresses uptake. Homeowners who feel the grant is a contractor bonus rather than a real saving will not participate.

**3. No bridge between assessment and action.**
A BER assessor visits, rates the home, recommends measures, and leaves. There is no fast, credible way to turn that recommendation into a project budget during or immediately after the visit. The window where a homeowner is most motivated to act closes before they have the information they need.

---

## How PlanitBER Solves This

PlanitBER generates an itemised, independent Bill of Quantities from DEAP survey geometry and a works selector. It is **not prepared by the contractor** — it is derived from the same data the BER assessor already holds.

**Solves problem 1 — cost certainty:**
Instant indicative budget, section by section, with county-adjusted labour rates and current Irish material prices. Homeowner leaves the assessment knowing what the upgrade will cost.

**Solves problem 2 — grant trust:**
The BOQ shows gross costs independently of any contractor. The grant deductions are shown line by line. The document explicitly states: *"Contractors should quote on the gross costs above — SEAI grants should reduce your invoice, not justify inflated pricing."* A homeowner who has an independent BOQ before engaging a contractor is in a fundamentally stronger position.

**Solves problem 3 — assessment to action:**
A BER assessor can generate a complete cost plan during the assessment visit and hand it to the homeowner alongside the BER certificate. The gap between recommendation and budget closes to zero.

---

## What It Produces

- Itemised BOQ with material rates, labour (county-adjusted), VAT — SCSI Cost Plan format
- Per-measure SEAI grant breakdown (Better Energy Homes or One Stop Shop)
- Net cost to homeowner after grants
- Estimated annual energy saving and payback years (from BER improvement)
- Green loan monthly repayment guide
- List of contractors needed with SEAI registration requirements
- Printable PDF suitable for bank and grant applications
- Actual vs. estimated cost tracker as works progress

## Why It Is Different

This is not a contractor quote. It is not a generic online estimator. It is an independent, geometry-driven cost plan aligned to DEAP measures, SEAI grant structure, and current Irish market rates — produced in the same format a quantity surveyor would use.
