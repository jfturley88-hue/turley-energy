## Basis of the figures

Every Estimated Spend figure is fully loaded: materials, labour at county rates, contractor's overhead and profit at 12%, contingency at 10%, and VAT at 13.5% (Solar PV at 0% per Revenue ruling, May 2023).

Indicative accuracy ±15–20% — to be evidenced in trial. Independent estimate — not prepared by any contractor.
